# Research Packet Assignment Prompt

Use when assigning a bounded research task to an agent or to yourself.

```text
Investigate exactly the packet below. Keep the scope bounded.

Do not silently make architectural decisions. Record observations, evidence,
unknowns, contradictions, and follow-up work.

Use these confidence labels:

- VERIFIED: directly established from code, configuration, source documents,
  tests, logs, or a repeatable experiment.
- INFERRED: strongly suggested by evidence but not traced or tested end-to-end.
- HYPOTHESIS: a candidate explanation to investigate.
- UNKNOWN: evidence is insufficient.

Packet:

Objective:
Question:
Related goal / frontier / decision:
Scope:
Starting evidence:
Method:
Required output:
Evidence standard:
Stopping condition:
Follow-up triggers:
```
